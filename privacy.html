Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version: focusroom has no server and no database of its own. It runs entirely in your browser, has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sells or shares your data. What you create is saved in your browser. If you turn on sync, your tasks, focus log, timer and water count are also saved in your own Google Drive, where only you and this app can reach it.

What's saved in your browser

Your settings, tasks, chosen theme, any images you upload as themes, the timer, the water you've ticked off today, and your focus log (the date, length and task name of each focus session) are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They're never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them. You can export or import a backup from Settings.

Syncing with your Google account

This is optional and off until you click Sync with Google in Settings, Tasks or Focus stats. The same Google account is the one you join study rooms with, and signing in to a room with a different account switches sync to that account. When you sync:

  • Google asks you to let focusroom use the drive.appdata permission. This only lets the app read and write its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It can't see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • focusroom saves three files there: focus-log.json, holding the date, length and task name of each focus session; tasks.json, holding your task list (each task's text, whether it's done, its estimate and the focus time spent on it); and state.json, holding the timer (its type, lengths and auto-start settings, and whether it's running and how far along it is) and how much water you ticked off each day over the last 30 days. Your other devices read and add to the same files when you sign in there.
  • focusroom also reads your Google account's email address and name, only to show which account you're signed in with and to keep each account's tasks and log separate.
  • Your browser talks to Google directly. The access token Google gives it is kept in memory only and expires after about an hour. Nothing passes through any server of ours.

To delete synced data: click Sign out in Settings, Tasks or Focus stats to stop syncing on a device (this also leaves any study room). To delete the files from Drive, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → focusroom → Options → Delete hidden app data. To withdraw the permission entirely, remove focusroom at your Google account's third-party connections.

focusroom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the sync feature described here. It isn't used for ads, isn't sold, and isn't transferred to anyone.

Study rooms and voice calls

  • To join a room you sign in with Google. Your Google sign-in token, which includes your name, email address and profile picture, is sent only to the other people in that room, over the room's end-to-end encrypted channel, so they can check who you are.
  • While you're in a room, the people in it see your name, picture, timer status, current task, today's focus total, and any chat messages and reactions you send. Voice audio goes directly between devices, encrypted, and is never recorded.
  • To connect, browsers use the public PeerJS broker to find each other, Google's STUN server to discover their network addresses, and, when a direct link isn't possible, a Metered relay. These services can see your IP address. The broker and relay only ever carry encrypted data they can't read.
  • Direct connections mean the other people in a room can learn your IP address. Only share invite links with people you trust.

Content from other services

Some parts of the app load content from other companies, which can see your IP address and apply their own privacy policies:

  • Live nature themes are video clips streamed from Mixkit.
  • Music plays in embedded players from YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com) or Spotify, only after you choose something to play. These players may set their own cookies.
  • Google sign-in loads Google's sign-in script from accounts.google.com when you use a room or sync.

focusroom itself sets no cookies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/avgrobo999/focus-room.
